# Human Mentality & Trader Psychology Framework

## Key Psychological Drivers
1. Greed & Impatience (Chasing gaps)
2. Fear & Hesitation (Missing valid entry signals)
3. Denial (Refusing to execute stops)
4. Revenge Trading (Doubling size after loss)

## System Constraints
The ZERO Terminal applies half-Kelly position sizing and strict CV-based calibration guardrails to remove emotion from execution.
