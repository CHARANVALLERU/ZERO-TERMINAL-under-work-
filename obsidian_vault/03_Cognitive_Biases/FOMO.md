# FOMO (Fear Of Missing Out)

## Trigger
Watching price rapidly move in one direction without being in the position.

## Cognitive Distortion
Belief that the asset will continue moving indefinitely without pullback, leading to entering at market high/low.

## Mitigations in ZERO
* Wait for price to touch the calculated ATR support/resistance levels.
* Force execution via POST_ONLY or DAY LIMIT orders via [[Nautilus_Order_Types]].
