# First Principles Thinking

## Definition
First Principles thinking requires breaking down a complex problem into its most fundamental truths and building an analysis from the ground up, rather than relying on analogy or conventional wisdom.

## Application in Trading
1. **Order Flow & Liquidity**: Price moves because of an imbalance between market buy and sell orders.
2. **Pre-Market Pricing**: Opening gap is driven by global futures, ADR premia, and overnight news sentiment.
3. **Risk Management**: Risk is defined by position size multiplied by stop distance.
