# Inversion

## Definition
Instead of asking 'How do I win this trade?', ask 'How could this trade turn into a catastrophic loss?' and systematically prevent those conditions.

## Checkpoints Before Order Execution
1. Is there an imminent high-impact macro announcement (RBI rate decision, US CPI)?
2. Am I entering near the predicted High envelope resistance?
3. Am I violating my daily max loss allocation?
