# QuantDinger Market Regimes

1. **TRENDING_BULLISH**: Gap Up > 0.35%, PCR >= 1.05, Positive Sentiment.
2. **TRENDING_BEARISH**: Gap Down < -0.35%, PCR <= 0.95, Negative Sentiment.
3. **VOLATILE_RANGEBOUND**: VIX > 22.0 or Range > 2.2%.
4. **MACRO_SHOCK**: Sentiment > 0.4 & VIX > 18.0.
5. **LOW_VOL_SQUEEZE**: Range < 1.0% & VIX < 14.0.
