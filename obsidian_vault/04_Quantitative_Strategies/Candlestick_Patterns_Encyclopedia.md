# Candlestick Patterns Encyclopedia

## Key Patterns Recognized
* **Bullish Engulfing**: Prior red body completely enclosed by large green candle. High reversal probability at support.
* **Hammer / Pinbar**: Long lower shadow with small upper body. Rejection of low prices.
* **Doji**: Open and Close virtually identical. Indicates indecision near range boundaries.
* **Shooting Star**: Long upper shadow at resistance indicating rejection of higher prices.
