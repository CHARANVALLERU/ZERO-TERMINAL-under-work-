# ZERO ENGINE — Candle Pattern Knowledge Base

## Classification System
Candle patterns are classified as: BULLISH, BEARISH, or NEUTRAL/CONTINUATION.
Context matters: a pattern's signal strength depends on the prior trend, volume, and key levels.

---

## SINGLE CANDLE PATTERNS

### Doji
- **Type**: NEUTRAL / Reversal warning
- **Description**: Open and close are nearly equal. Represents indecision in the market.
- **Variants**: Standard Doji, Long-Legged Doji, Gravestone Doji, Dragonfly Doji
- **Gravestone Doji**: Long upper shadow, close near low → BEARISH at tops
- **Dragonfly Doji**: Long lower shadow, close near high → BULLISH at bottoms
- **Trading Psychology**: Bulls and bears are in balance. Next candle direction is critical confirmation. Weak hands are trapped on both sides.
- **ZERO Signal**: Watch for breakout of doji high/low with volume confirmation.

### Hammer
- **Type**: BULLISH reversal (at bottom of downtrend)
- **Description**: Small body, long lower shadow (2x body), little/no upper shadow.
- **Inverted Hammer**: Same shape but inverted — needs next-day bullish confirmation.
- **Trading Psychology**: Sellers pushed price down aggressively but buyers recovered. Shows demand absorption at lows. Institutional accumulation signature.
- **ZERO Signal**: Entry on breakout above hammer high. Stop below hammer low.

### Hanging Man
- **Type**: BEARISH reversal (at top of uptrend)
- **Description**: Same shape as Hammer but appears at the top. Warns of distribution.
- **Trading Psychology**: Smart money is selling into retail buying. The recovery from lows is weak, suggesting distribution not accumulation.
- **ZERO Signal**: Confirm with next bearish candle. Enter short below hanging man low.

### Shooting Star
- **Type**: BEARISH reversal (at top of uptrend)
- **Description**: Small body near the low, long upper shadow (2x body), little/no lower shadow.
- **Trading Psychology**: Bulls pushed price higher but sellers overwhelmed them. Distribution zone. Trapped longs become fuel for the decline.
- **ZERO Signal**: Short below shooting star low. Strong if near resistance or Fibonacci level.

### Spinning Top
- **Type**: NEUTRAL — Indecision
- **Description**: Small body with roughly equal upper and lower shadows.
- **Trading Psychology**: Neither bulls nor bears in control. Often precedes a trend change.
- **ZERO Signal**: Wait for breakout candle to determine direction.

### Marubozu (Full)
- **Type**: STRONG directional candle
- **Bullish Marubozu**: Opens at low, closes at high. No shadows. Pure bull power.
- **Bearish Marubozu**: Opens at high, closes at low. No shadows. Pure bear power.
- **Trading Psychology**: Complete dominance by one side. No indecision. Often starts or accelerates a trend.
- **ZERO Signal**: Trend-following entry. Confirms breakout validity.

## DUAL CANDLE PATTERNS

### Bullish Engulfing
- **Type**: BULLISH reversal
- **Description**: Small bearish candle followed by a large bullish candle that completely engulfs the prior body.
- **Trading Psychology**: Bears in full control then completely overpowered. Institutional buying overpowers retail selling.
- **ZERO Signal**: Enter on close of engulfing candle or open of next. Volume spike confirms.

### Bearish Engulfing
- **Type**: BEARISH reversal
- **Description**: Small bullish candle followed by large bearish candle engulfing the prior body.
- **Trading Psychology**: Bulls exhausted, distribution by institutions. Retail trapped long.
- **ZERO Signal**: Short on close. Target prior support. Stop above engulfing candle high.

### Bullish Harami
- **Type**: BULLISH reversal (weaker than engulfing)
- **Description**: Large bearish candle followed by a small bullish candle contained within the first body.
- **Trading Psychology**: Selling momentum slowing. Bears losing control. Needs confirmation.
- **ZERO Signal**: Wait for next bullish candle to confirm. Not a standalone trade.

### Bearish Harami
- **Type**: BEARISH reversal (weaker signal)
- **Description**: Large bullish candle followed by a small bearish candle within its body.
- **Trading Psychology**: Bullish momentum fading. Watch for follow-through.
- **ZERO Signal**: Confirmation required. Short below second candle low.

### Piercing Line
- **Type**: BULLISH reversal
- **Description**: Bearish candle, then bullish candle opens below prior low but closes above the midpoint of prior bearish body.
- **Trading Psychology**: Strong demand absorption. Buyers defending the bottom aggressively.
- **ZERO Signal**: Entry above second candle high. Reliable at major support zones.

### Dark Cloud Cover
- **Type**: BEARISH reversal
- **Description**: Bullish candle, then bearish candle opens above prior high and closes below midpoint of prior bullish body.
- **Trading Psychology**: Sellers overwhelming buyers at the top. Distribution signal.
- **ZERO Signal**: Short below second candle low. Common near resistance levels.

### Tweezer Tops / Bottoms
- **Type**: Reversal
- **Tweezer Top**: Two candles with matching highs — BEARISH at uptrend peak
- **Tweezer Bottom**: Two candles with matching lows — BULLISH at downtrend bottom
- **Trading Psychology**: Price failed twice at the same level. Double rejection signal. Clean institutional level.

## TRIPLE CANDLE PATTERNS

### Morning Star
- **Type**: STRONG BULLISH reversal
- **Structure**: Bearish candle → small body/doji (gap down) → bullish candle closing above 50% of first candle
- **Trading Psychology**: Panic selling followed by indecision followed by institutional buying. Three-phase bottoming process.
- **ZERO Signal**: Strong entry signal. Enter on close of third candle. High reliability at key support.

### Evening Star
- **Type**: STRONG BEARISH reversal
- **Structure**: Bullish candle → small body/doji (gap up) → bearish candle closing below 50% of first candle
- **Trading Psychology**: Distribution completing. Three-phase topping process. Institutional selling into retail euphoria.
- **ZERO Signal**: Short on close of third candle. High reliability at key resistance.

### Three White Soldiers
- **Type**: STRONG BULLISH continuation/reversal
- **Description**: Three consecutive large bullish candles, each closing higher with small shadows.
- **Trading Psychology**: Sustained institutional accumulation. Buyers in full control across multiple sessions.
- **ZERO Signal**: Momentum entry. Pullbacks to first candle high become support.

### Three Black Crows
- **Type**: STRONG BEARISH continuation/reversal
- **Description**: Three consecutive large bearish candles, each closing lower with small shadows.
- **Trading Psychology**: Sustained distribution. Sellers systematically overwhelm buyers.
- **ZERO Signal**: Momentum short. Rallies to first candle low become resistance.

### Three Inside Up
- **Type**: BULLISH reversal (confirmed Harami)
- **Structure**: Bearish → Bullish Harami → Bullish confirmation candle closing above first candle's midpoint

### Three Inside Down
- **Type**: BEARISH reversal (confirmed Harami)
- **Structure**: Bullish → Bearish Harami → Bearish confirmation closing below first candle's midpoint

## NIFTY/BANKNIFTY SPECIFIC NOTES

- **Pre-market (9:00-9:15 AM IST)**: Opening candle pattern sets tone for 30-60 minutes
- **Opening Range (9:15-9:45 AM)**: First 30-min candle pattern is highest significance
- **10:30 AM Reset**: Common inflection — check candle pattern after 10:30 reversal
- **2:00-2:30 PM**: Institutional position adjustment — watch for reversal patterns
- **3:00 PM**: Last hour volatility — patterns unreliable, use range extremes only
